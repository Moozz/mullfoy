🔎 Decision tree

1 · Is it food or garden waste?
Peels, scraps, coffee, tea bags, plants, flowers 🟩 Green
▼ no
2 · Is it paper or cardboard?
Clean & dry — newspaper, box, letter, egg carton, boarding pass 🟦 Blue
Wet, greasy, used with food 🟩 Green
Coated, waxed or thermal receipt ⬛ Black
▼ no
3 · ⚠️ Stop — chemical, battery or LED?
Paint, solvent, cleaner, pesticide, full spray can, batteries, LED & energy lamps, tubes, waste oil ☠️ Hazard
▼ no
4 · Was it packaging — did it hold a product?
Glass bottle or jar🫙 Glass
Plastic, metal, foil (even with food residue), carton, paper+plastic composites🟨 Yellow
▼ no
5 · Everything else
Plastic objects, hygiene, ceramics, drinking glasses, ash, sponges, wood, textile labels⬛ Black
